What is the inrush current during transformer energization?

Transformer inrush energisation studies play a crucial role in understanding the behaviour of transformers during their startup process. These studies help in identifying and analyzing the inrush current phenomenon that occurs when a transformer is first energized. By studying the inrush current, we can assess the impact on the transformer and surrounding equipment, and make informed decisions to mitigate any potential risks.
