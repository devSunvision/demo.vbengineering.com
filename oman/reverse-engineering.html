Reverse Engineering

Reverse Engineering is a term coined when an existing equipment is taken apart and analysed on how it functions , possibility of enhancement and redesign the equipment with the enhancements and cost effective ways is carried out. The redesign requires a perfect knowledge of the existing design, it's functionality so as to enhance it for the required application. VB Engineering is a reverse Engineering Consultant offering their services at Oman . VB Engineers has an expertise in redesigning with a care on legal and patent related concerns.

The data acquisition is achieved by 3D scanners, that enable a 3D point cloud model . 3D scanners give a comprehensive details of the contact and non contact type equipment. The 3D measurements of the equipment is achieved through 3D scan. With the measurements and the software that provide the reverse engineering tools a 3D drawing is developed. In many cases even when a 3D model of the existing model is not available, VB Engineering excels in the development of a 3D drawing that forms the basis for the reverse engineering.

Laser scanning is the technical tool that acquires the data for a non contact type of equipment. The data provided by the laser scan is analysed and with the expertise in data analysis, mathematical and engineering grasp at VB Engineering and with thorough testing taking the practical issues, and the new technologies and ever changing market requisites into consideration 3D drafting is developed.. Reverse engineering is opted when a new equipment at a less cost and enhanced features is needed.

Some industries may not be requiring the high-end technology or the design constraints like the grade of the material used, the consumption of power and other physical operating conditions be realised by reverse engineering. The testing phase enables a reverse engineer in checking with the various possibilities and demonstrate them with the help of a computer model and document the outputs and finally creating the draft.

With the developed 3D drafting the 3D model of the reverse engineered equipment is developed. The enhanced Mechanical Design gives the base for speeding up the time required for the development of the equipment . Reverse Engineered developed model may have to overcome the issues related to ethical and legal related issues. But it is justified by the fact that the dynamically changing industrial products and the advancements in the equipment that may have to be incorporated in the present equipment is possible by the replacement of the existing equipment. This might not be feasible always on the part of the management and this is where reverse engineered models and equipment that are more precisely developed with the help of 3D laser scanner finds application. VB Engineering offers services satisfying the customers' requirements giving them an optimised, economic and reliable model.
