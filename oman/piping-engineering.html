Piping Engineering

Oil and Natural gas, Petroleum companies, electrical power generation companies and other manufacturing and production companies have a necessity of interconnection of equipment through pipes. And the operating conditions vary from facility to facility. Piping Engineering is all about this interconnection in a systematic way and ensure the flow properly. Unlike the common reasoning, the role of a piping engineer is not limited to just the designing or installation phase but also throughout the operating conditions so as to avoid hazardous fluid leakages, explosions having fatal effects on the working personnel etc. Many a times the pipes pose a tendency to fail causing damage to other equipment, injuries to operating personnel, and extended halt in the work causing a time delay and affecting the productivity. Thus the Piping Design has to follow high technical considerations before initialising them in the practical operating conditions.

A proper Piping design ensures reliability of the facility and safe working conditions. Piping Engineering is a branch that considers the prominence of piping . A piping Engineer plays the role of determining the piping design taking care that the design codes are followed with taking into consideration the company requirements, location of the facility, standards of the materials used , operating conditions, stress on the pipes, safety of the operating personnel. Piping Engineer at VB Engineering ensures that the ASME B31 codes are followed in the piping design.

Piping drawing is a pictorial representation of the interconnection of the equipment through the pipe, depicting the joints, indicating the stress, pressure, dead weight in case of pipes with large diameters, thermal hammer, surges that occur during the working conditions. Pipes are subjected to expansions and contractions with the variations in temperatures, corrosion and embrittlement are other factors that pose a concern for piping engineer in designing and deciding the piping life .

Pipe draft and design at VB Engineering is carried out by following the standards proposed by ASME,OSHA and the laws imposed by the Sultanate of Oman taking care that the client requirements are met in providing

the most economic and standard design with component details, corrosion allowance, material specifications, provided in the drawing . Our well experienced piping engineers provide the clients with a 3D model of the system basing on the pipe stress analysis that is the most optimised design with indications to the expected operating conditions , required maintenance of the pipes, choice of valves, joints, welding types ,safety measures to be followed, on shore or off shore structure of the facility, seismic activity of the ground etc. 3D modelling enables to correct the design if necessary, change the piping in accordance with the new advancements in piping technology , addition of new equipment or module in the facility. We at VB Engineering understand the heavy investment that is met by the companies and hence provide a worthy design and a safe pipeline even under working conditions that vary in temperature, pressure, surges etc.
