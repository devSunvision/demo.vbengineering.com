What is Arc Flash Risk Assessment or Arc Flash Study?

Qatar is one of the countries with promising safety standards in the industries, refinaries, hospitals, banking firms. Electrical energy is the common pedestal for all these companies. With the utilization of Electrical energy comes the possibility of faults like Short circuit Electrical Faults, Open circuit faults, overload circuit faults, Electrical surges and their consequences along with the advantages and multiple applications of Electrical Energy. One of the faults of concern is the one that leads to Arc Flash Accident or Arc Blast between the conductors.

Arc Flash Accident, a phenomenon that occurs in electrical networks under fault conditions. Electrical equipment and the electrical networks are prone to Arc Flash accidents independent of their operating voltages, low, medium or high voltages. As known the fault in an electrical network results in heavy currents of the range of 10KA to 125KA flow within the network posing a high wreckage not only to the other electrical equipment but also to the working personnel. Thus the Arc Flash study and analysis conducted by Arc Flash companies like VB Engineering plays a pivotal role in the area of safety for the facilites. Being aware of the possibility of electrical faults leading to Arc Flash which is sometimes termed as Flash over caused between two conductors when there is a breakdown of the insulating ,material . The high amounts of currents are accompanied by high temperatures that melts and vaporizes the metal and causing an air gap between the conductors. The air gap under Arc Flash conditions is ionised and provides a conducting path and thus sustain the Arc Flash.
