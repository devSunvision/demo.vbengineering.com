Our Proven Methodology

1. Comprehensive Site Visit & Data Collection
Our expert team conducts a meticulous site visit to gather foundational ground data before the scanning process begins. We perform a detailed presurvey at the steel plant, establishing a precise roadmap and schedule that aligns with operational needs. This groundwork ensures that every phase of the project is well-planned and executed with minimal disruption to ongoing operations.

2. 3D Laser Scanning & Point Cloud Data Acquisition
We leverage cutting-edge point cloud technology to capture highly accurate as-built data from existing steel plant structures. Our advanced 3D laser scanners, with accuracy up to 4mm, are strategically positioned throughout the facility to generate detailed sub-models. The colored, high-resolution 3D models not only simplify complex data but also optimize file sizes for seamless management, enhancing project planning and execution.



3. Post-Processing & Digital Twin Development
The collected sub-models are expertly processed using advanced point cloud techniques, resulting in a cohesive and accurate 3D primitive model. Our digital twin development process incorporates this data to create a virtual replica of the steel factory, offering unparalleled accuracy for facility management. The final digital twin is a fully operational digital asset, ready to support maintenance planning, safety assessments, and operational efficiency.
