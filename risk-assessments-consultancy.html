A risk assessment is a crucial step in workplace safety management. It involves identifying potential hazards and evaluating the level of risk they pose to employees. Our team of risk management practitioners will work closely with your organization to conduct a comprehensive evaluation of your workplace and develop a tailored risk assessment plan.

During the risk assessment process, we will:

Determine the requirement for specific risk assessments while conducting Health & Safety Audits and Inspections.

Design and develop Risk Assessor training for scheduled employees.
Deliver various risk-assessment templates covering a wide range of activities.
Conduct specialist risk assessments to address unique workplace hazards.
Ensure that employees are aware of risk-control measures.
Regularly review and update the risk assessment to adapt to changing circumstances.
Our approach to risk assessment goes beyond mere paperwork. We are committed to identifying real risks and implementing relevant and appropriate risk control measures. By partnering with VB® Engineering, you can create a safer workplace for your employees and reduce the likelihood of accidents and injuries.
