Arc Flash Study Standards & Compliance


Causes of Arc Flash can be a failure in the operation of the protective devices, Working on an energised line, accumulation of severe dust and dirt creating a conductive path, an undesired path for the fault current, unprofessional handling of the equipment, faulty switching practices, operating tools handling by the operating personnel. The Arc if quenched in a prescribed time is not hazardous. But when its not mitigated in the stipulated time it leads to many a hazards listed above. Untrained working personnel with little knowledge on the hazardous environment, or the potential of voltage and current levels that he is working, careless practices of required PPE , ignorance on part of the operating personnel as to the incident energy, safe operating distances, unable to identify Arc Flash Hazard working conditions. These are some of the main reasons in severe injuries and fatalities in Arc Flash Incident. In all such cases the level of hazard remains to be grave and leads to heavy fines on part of the management, hospital expenses, liability in insurance claims, property damage and capital costs. These account to be in millions of Dirhams. Hence the regulatory bodies like OSHA 29 CFR 1910, NFPA 70 E, IEEE 1584- 2018, IEC-60079-17 emphasise on an Arc Flash Study to be carried out periodically and to implement the Arc Flash Study Requirements invariably.
Some of the after effects of an Arc Flash are:
Electric shock
Severe burns
Arc Flash leading to temporary or permanent blindness
Arc Blasts

Arc Flash Study Analysis

With the acquired data, our team of experts develop a single line diagram using SKM power tools, ETAP. Develop models with the implementation of novel softwares and technologies like NEPLAN, EDSA, Cymdist, Arc Pro. The SLD preparation takes into consideration all the equipment of about 30 KW and more, sources, Buses, Power generators, transmission and distribution networks, type of grounding implemented, capacitors, reactors, MCCs, control panels, switch boards, type pf protective devices, fuses, motors operating at voltage levels of 600V, 400V. It is widely seen that Arc Flash occurs at voltage levels of 400 V and other high voltages. Hence the data corresponding to operating voltages of less than 240 V is not taken into consideration except in cases where it poses a 120 KA, or other low impedance loads that may cause Arc Flash Hazard. The development of SLDs also prove to be advantageous in identifying unused or unnecessary establishments in the utility or the possibility of expansion for future use. At VB Engineering we provide you with panel wise drawings for easy assessment complying with the international standards and Arc Flash study requirements.

The Arc Flash Calculation is the main aspect of Arc Flash Analysis. IEEE 1584-2018 has formulated specific models in calculation of bolted fault current, Incident energy, Arc current, normalised incident energy, RMS values of bolted fault currents, frequencies of operation, operating system voltage, distance of operation, gap between the conductors where the Arc occurs, type of enclosures, types of electrodes deployed, types of phases and grounding as per the standards.

Arc Flash Calculation

Calculations pertaining to Arcing Current, normalised incident energy, incident energy based on the data of types cables, MCCs, control panels, switchgear, enclosure types implementing the formulas formulated by IEEE standards.

The TCC characteristics depicting the clearing time, tripping time. Protective relay characteristics depicting the delay time, relay operating time are used to determine the short circuit coordination study.

Arc Flash Hazard Analysis confers the information about the Arc Flash Boundaries of operation with and without PPE. An Arc Flash or Flash over is an unwanted and unforeseen danger that has the possibility of occurrence at a wide range of facilities irrespective of its size. A 1-phase or a 3-phase fault occurring at any point of the facility may lead to an Arc Flash. The fault causes a fault current and an undesired discharge between two conductors. And when this discharge is supplied with a sufficient amount of energy it leads to an ionisation of the air around it further creating a plasma along with high amounts of heat energy, light and sound. The deafening sound of about 160 DB causes rupture in eardrums. The heat energy associated with an Arc Blast is of 35000 Fahrenheit. The Flash of light may cause blindness to the operating personal. The Arc Blast leads to the melting and explosion of the surrounding equipment releasing the molten metal and shrapnel with a tons of pressure. Arc Flash Hazards lead to death and severe burns to the operating personnel.
