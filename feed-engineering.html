Introduction to Feed Engineering

It is most important to understand and reflect the client's exact requirements and project requirements in the FEED activity to avoid a big change in the EPC phase. The FEED Work takes about more than 1 year in the case of a large-sized project such as a GAS plant. It is important to maintain close and clear communication with the client, and client stations at the Contractor’s office during the work execution. Many studies shown that the FEED can reduce the cost 2 to 30% at the stage of design and execution. FEED is also known as Front End Loading or Front End Engineering.
