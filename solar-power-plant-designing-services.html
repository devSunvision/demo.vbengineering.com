Solar Power Plant Life-Time

The useful life of any well maintained Solar Power plant is considered to be 25 years. Solar Power plants will be used beyond 25 years while power generation will be less. Many Solar Panel manufacturers guarantees the output of 90% up to 10 years and 80% up to 25 years.

1MW solar power plant’s Area

To generate 1mw solar power using Crystalline technology, minimum 4.5-5 acres land will be required and minimum 6.5-7.5 acres using Thin-Film technology. This will be the only a rough benchmark and may vary based on different technology and efficiency of panels.
