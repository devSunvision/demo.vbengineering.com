What are the results of arc flash? A. Arc flash can damage infrastructure, electrical equipment , and even may injure people severely . Arc flash is very dangerous which has to be prevented as early as possible. The longer the arc the severe the damage.

What are the general requirements as per OSHA standards? A. All places of employment, passageways, storerooms, service centers are to be kept clean and orderly in sanitary condition. The floor of every room shall be kept clean and if possible in dry condition. To facilitate cleaning every room shall be kept clean from protruding nails, holes , splitters. Permanent aisles and passageways shall be marked properly. Floor loading protection to be implemented.

What is the purpose of IEEE1584? A. IEEE 1584-2002 was developed to help protect people from arc-flash hazard dangers. The predicted arc current and incident energy are used in selecting appropriate overcurrent protective devices and personal protective equipment(generally abbreviated as PPE), as well as defining safe working distance. Since the magnitude of the arc current is inherently linked with the degree of arc hazard, the arc is examined as a circuit parameter.

How to protect from shock and arc flash? A. We can protect from arc flash and shock by doing justification for live work. Work permits are to be implemented in order to do effective maintenance and also protection from shock and arc flash. Approach boundaries like limited boundary, restricted boundary are a better way to protect from shock and arc flash.
