Equipment Evaluation

The equipment technical evaluation process adheres to a systematic approach to guarantee the precision and dependability of the assessment. This evaluation plays a vital role in the M&A process, ensuring that equipment meets stringent quality and safety standards, while also providing transparency for investors.

  1. Home
  2. Equipment Evaluation

Site Survey and Information Collection

Gathering information about the equipment and operation system, reviewing system design, technical documentation, and specifications, and conducting a site survey.



Inspection and Operation Test at the Site

Carrying out visual inspections, conducting system test runs, considering the surroundings, and interviewing operators and other relevant personnel on-site.



Equipment Technical Evaluation Report


Consolidating test and trial system results, developing a comprehensive technical assessment report, and providing recommendations to investors based on the evaluation findings.
