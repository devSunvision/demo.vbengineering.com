The ten power system studies.

VB Engineering delivers the full IEEE Color Books practice. Each study is built on a reconciled single-line, run on the appropriate main platform, and validated against operating-company standards.

Load Flow Analysis

Steady-state operation under all switching configurations. Identifies voltage drop, equipment overload, reactive compensation needs. ETAP-modelled.

Short Circuit Analysis

Fault current at every bus across multiple fault types. Verifies equipment interrupting capacity. IEC 60909 or ANSI methodology.

Arc Flash to IEEE 1584-2018

Incident energy at every working position. Arc flash boundary. PPE category. Bilingual labels installed under witness signature.

Protection Coordination

Time-current curves for every relay. Selectivity at every operating scenario. Backup tripping margins. Tested against operating-company codes.

Transient + Dynamic Stability

Rotor angle, voltage, and frequency response to disturbances. DigSILENT-modelled. Critical for high-fault and high-renewable plants.

Harmonic Analysis

VFD, rectifier, and converter harmonics. IEEE 519 compliance check. Filter sizing if needed. PSCAD-modelled for power electronics.

Motor Starting

Voltage dip during large motor starting. Acceleration time. Bus voltage profile during sequential starts. Critical for utility intake design.

Insulation Coordination

BIL withstand at every voltage level. Lightning and switching surge analysis. Surge arrester selection per IEC 60071.

Reliability + Availability

Failure rate and mean-time-to-repair at the system level. Critical contingencies identified. Spare strategy informed by quantitative gaps.

The main platforms.

ETAP

Primary platform for steady-state, short circuit, arc flash, and protection. Native to ANSI and IEC. Deep operating-company adoption.

DigSILENT PowerFactory

Primary platform for transient stability, dynamic simulation, and protection coordination. Strong for transmission and high-renewable systems.

PSCAD

Primary platform for harmonic analysis, converter studies, EMT-level transients. Required for HVDC, VFD-heavy industrial, and STATCOM work.
