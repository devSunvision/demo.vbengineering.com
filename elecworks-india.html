Elecworks

Product Description

    Elecworks is a powerful new generation software tool, that enables electrical designers and automation experts to create automation and electrical installation projects. Through Windows standard interfaces, engineers can produce better installation designs and supporting documentation faster. Main Functionalities: Schematic design tool, draw multiple lines, insert symbols with an automatic break in the line. Multi-standard symbol library. Layout library, expert copy of sheets and projects. Automatic generation of terminal strips and their management. Management of I/O and PLCs management.
