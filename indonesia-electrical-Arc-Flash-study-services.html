Arc Flash Study Analysis

Arc Flash is an accidental event that can occur at any facility like an industrial, commercial, residential systems when the short circuit current flows in an inadvertent path and gets energised by the ionized air surrounding the conductors which now acts as a dielectric. This Arc current of the range of 10 KA to 30 KA causes a blast of plasma and an enormous heat and light and a pressure wave sending molten metal, and other debris in the vicinity with a loud sound of about 160 DB. The toxic gases, shrapnel and the heat energy cause severe burns, visual and acoustic damages and may even cause death. Statistics show that Arc Flash incidents occur quite often with severe injuries.

One of the most important information from the data acquired is the value of short circuit current in Arc Flash hazard analysis. The short circuit currents at all the equipment for different modes of operation is collected. And for each mode of operation and at each equipment the possibility of an Arc Flash hazard, effects of Arc Flash and the severity and the potential damage that could result is deduced. As per IEEE 1584-2018 mathematical models, the data of the conductors, type of conductors, gap between the conductors, the equipment where the conductors are installed, Arcing fault current values, type of enclosure like horizontal, vertical, box type, HOA, VCB, VCBB, VOA, are to be obtained from the facility. Depending on the voltage range, short circuit analysis is determined.

Arc Flash Safety Scope

With the data collected and SLD developed, the fault currents and bolted fault currents are calculated with the implementation of the mathematical models provided by IEEE 1584- 2018. The values of bolted fault currents and Arc fault currents are usseful in Arc Flash calculation. For all modes of operation like multiple utility feeders running concurrently at a given point of time, one or more primary feeders, two transformers with tie open or closed, MCC, and for different X/R ratio the currents are determined.

Arc Flash calculation further requires the Incident Energy values at each equipment likely to exhibit an Arc Flash hazard. The severity of an Arc Flash and thus the Arc Flash hazard analysis is determined from the incident energy value. The Arc duration, gap between the conductors are the major factors on which the Arc Flash hazard depends. The Arc duration, protective relay operating times are all obtained from the TCC curves. TCC curves for circuit breakers, fuses and their overlapping are determined. The short circuit coordination study is determined from the TCC curves, settings of the protective devices and all the branch currents that energise the Arc fault current. From the values obtained from the short circuit coordination studies and Arc fault currents and bolted fault currents, incident energy values, the Arc Flash Hazard is categorised. HRC provides the operating distance for the corresponding incident energy, enclosure type and gap between the conductors.
