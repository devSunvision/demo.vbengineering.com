Data Collection

Our certified and trained electrical team will attend the facility as per the schedule. They will perform the data collection panel wise and capture the respective images using VB Tantra mobile app. They digitize the collected data to make accessible to back-office team simultaneously. They also collect any further data provided by the client to analyze the system, which are not available directly from equipment name plate details.

System design and Analysis

Our dedicated back-end team first verify and compare the collected data with captured images. Once the data is found as per the ground reality, they move further for designing. Using reputed computer softwares like ETAP/SKM taking the system’s collected data as input, we design the system and run for load flow studies. Our in-depth analysis considers various oil rig configurations like Deep Drilling, Shallow Drilling, jacking, maintenance operations for study. We also analyze equipment with standing capacity and the percentage loading for all configuration and loading patterns.

Recommendations and implementation

Our expertise team review the system, provide the clients with various loading patterns, loss calculations, voltage drops, voltage available at each bus and load current values for the network and provide detailed report along with recommendations. We also analyze equipment with standing capacity and the percentage loading for all configuration and loading patterns and provide detailed report along with recommendations. Our load flow analysis report not only provide the present status but also provides the scope availability for future enhancements.
