Arc Flash & Shock Boundary Calculations



What is an Arc Flash study or Arc Flash Risk Assessment ? This is the question on every safety engineers mind when they come across OSHA and NFPA 70E requirements. Before understanding what is Arc Flash analysis ? It is important to know actually what is an electric Arc ?

Electrical Arc Flash or Arc Flash Risk is an electrical hazard present in the system which causes very dangerous electrical and fire accidents. When the electric Arc Flash accident happens in the system it generates huge amount of fire which may even cause death to the working personnel. This is the reason OSHA considers Arc Flash hazards as very dangerous risk that needs to be analyzed and mitigated. Here arises the requirement of Arc Flash hazard analysis and mitigation by proper Arc Flash risk assessment of the system. As per OSHA and NFPA-70E, analyzing the short circuit currents in the system and calculating the incident energy levels, creating the warning labels with the details of the incident energy levels, approach boundaries details, Arc Flash shock hazard details are the Arc Flash hazard analysis requirements.

Digitization & Modelling

We need detailed information of the electrical installations for Arc Flash Risk Assessment. We have inhouse team who are trained and experienced in collecting the data required for Arc Flash Study. The data collection and digitization team will ensure they capture every detail of the electrical installation ensuring the Arc Flash Analysis study results the best output. They will collect the required data panel wise i.e., incoming service voltage, equipment rated current, bus voltages, cable sizes, breaker details, transformer ratings etc., along with their images.We digitize the data to make accessible to the back-end team simultaneously to reduce the latency. Analysis & Assessment

Post data collection the data collected for the Arc Flash Study will be verified and digitized by our back-office team. We have a dedicated team to verify and validate the data collected with the actual site conditions. Our quality and inspection team will coordinate with data collection team and client to compare the collected data with images captured. The data undergoes multilevel scrutiny to ensure the correctness. Once the data is approved by client our team of engineers starts preparing the engineering model of the data in ETAP or PTW SKM software as per client’s choice for the detailed analysis.

We perform the power system studies on the engineering model todetermine short circuit values like bolted short circuit and arcing short circuit values in the network. Our team will study the protection provided to the system in coordination with the load and fault values of the network. Engineering model will be used to calculatethe incident energy values at every bus and we perform a detailed Arc Flash study in compliance with OSHA, NFPA 70E 2021, IEEE 1584 and NEC latest standards.The detailed Arc Flash study results include incident energy values, Arc Flash boundaries, Approach boundaries like limited approach boundary, Restricted approach boundary, shock hazard details, working model details like PPE required and safe distance and short circuit values.
