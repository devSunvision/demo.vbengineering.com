Arc Flash Study Standards & Compliance

Arc Flash is a phenomenon that occurs when current flows in inadvertent path resulting in an Arc. The causes of this Arc are accumulation of dirt making it a conductive path, choice of operating tools by the operating personnel, faulty handling of the switchgear, or other switching activities. One elucidation to avoid the formation of the Arc is to deenergise the line or Bus being operated that are accompanied by a downtime leading to a loss in hundreds of Taiwanese Dollars. Thus many firms are incredulous to deenergise the lines. This Arc if quenched swiftly proves to be posing no danger. But many a practical cases prove that when the Arc is energised sufficiently it results in a Flashover Or simply an Arc Flash with a high energy of both light and heat being released in all possible directions ionizing the air around it further energising the Arc. The energy melts the metals around it sending a burst of molten metal at a pressure of about half a ton making the person in the proximity to be injured severely. The person may experience an Electric shock , burn injuries, blindness due to the bright light from the Flashover or a severe damage to the eardrums due to the high sound of about 160 DB which is way too higher than the normal range a human can bear. At times an Arc Flash may lead to death or painful injuries needing a prolonged time to heal. It so happens that all these series of events occur in a fraction of a second, leaving no time for the operating personnel to escape for protection. Having the right PPE, knowledge of Arc Flash possibility through effective signs and labels have proven to be safeguarding the workers under such dangerous Arc Flash.
