Arc Flash Hazards & Mitigations

Arc Flash Boundary distance is the distance between the operating personnel and the Arc Flash point. Arc Flash protection boundaries are determined by the standards set by NFPA 70 E where the operating personnel may suffer second degree burns without PPE known as the Flash Protection boundary. So as to avoid injuries operating personnel are advised to employ the required PPE. Nominal voltage values are used to determine the limited approach boundary. Limited approach boundary is nearer than Flash protection boundary. Restricted approach boundary is the distance where a qualified working personnel can approach. Restricted approach boundary is the exposed energised part of the circuit and is nearer than limited approach boundary. Required PPE so as to work in this distance. Prohibited approach boundary where the exposed live part of the circuit is much nearer than the restricted approach boundary and mandatory requirement of an operating personnel that is appropriately qualified and with the required PPE. Arc Flash hazard risk categories are determined from the incidentenergy and Arc Flash boundaries. All the calculations are the anticipated values and the realvalues during an incident of an Arc Flash may be even higher than the calculated values. Theprotective devices are to be selected with a higher tolerance levels than the calculated values to obtain a coordinated protective device operation. VB Engineering in accordance tothe safety regulations proposed by NFPA 70 E, IEEE 1584-2018, OSHA, NFPA 70 B, NESCdetermines the HRC based on incident energy and suggests the required PPE for each category. Arc Flash mitigation methods are proposed based on the Arc Flash hazard calculation

Arc Flash Study Analysis

The data from the facility as per the documents and records is collected so as to ensure all the equipment in the facility are included. The documentation as built in electrical drawings enables to identify the possible hazardous equipment.
A field inspection team from VB Engineering conducts the survey in detail. A technical and well experienced team identifies any additional equipment that might be not included in the records and documents. Also a field inspection helps in acquiring in detail with all the devices feeders, circuit breakers, protective devices, motors, generators, name plate details of all the equipment, types, sizes, time settings, cables and their properties, impedances, X/R ratios, transformer tap changers, transformer KVA/MVA ratings, types of loads, loading information, types of conductors, air gap distance between the conductors, types of electrodes employed, types of enclosures and all other data on field is collected.
An onsite data collection helps in Arc Flash Hazard Analysis with more practical scenarios as the physical verification encompasses
the details like the working environment, deterioration of the equipment, accumulation of dirt and dust, possibility of humidity and thus moisture accumulation, loose contacts, maintenance levels of the equipment, any infestation of rodents etc., proper functioning of connectors, clamps and other switch gear possibility of any wear and tear over the period of operations. With this thorough data at hand leads to identify any unused or unintended protective devices or switch gear present, equipment rated above the actual load requirements, equipment running on overloaded conditions such that they can be removed or replaced by other equipment and get the job done and also in identifying the call for new equipment and improve the system reliability, and productivity of the facility.

Arc Flash Hazard Solutions and Report

With the real time data obtained by field survey and electrical drawings, the protective relays and the time settings of all the relays at each equipment is considered. Calculate the Arc Flash Hazard Analysis with the most probable assumptions of the Arc Flash Hazard at each equipment and conduct Short circuit coordination study. The short circuit currents are determined at each equipment. Branch currents that contribute to the Arc currents are calculated. The duty ratings, relay tripping times, fault clearing time of the protective devices along with the time current curves helps in arbitrating the short circuit coordination study at each equipment. At VB Engineering with the aid of SKM Power tools, Easy Power, Arc Pro and other software taking into account the current operating practices and the possible assumptions of Arc Flash Risk Short Circuit analysis and short circuit coordination of the protective devices is evaluated. The coordinated protective device operation is desirable. The short circuit coordination study suggests the settings for the protective devices to be decisive which protective devices i.e., upstream or downstream, protective devices are to operate for the given Arc fault current.

Arc Flash Calculation

From the short circuit analysis Arc Flash calculation is carried about. IEEE 1584-2018 and NFPA have suggested calculation models and methods for Arc Flash Calculation. Arc Flash Calculation is to determine the incident energy and Arc fault current from the data at the facility. Incident energy is the released during an Arc Flash per unit area on a surface. Incident energy is the key value in determining the Arc Flash hazard analysis. Incident energy is directly proportional to the bolted fault current and inversely proportional to the distance of operation from the point of Arc Flash squared. Thus, the incident and normalized incident energy calculations are significant in determination of Arc Flash Hazard Analysis, Arc Flash calculation and Arc Flash hazard assessment. With SKM power tools, a large number of buses can be evaluated and Arc Flash calculation is determined. The incident energy calculation is also pragmatic in determination of the Arc Flash boundaries.
