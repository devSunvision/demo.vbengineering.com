Simulation Solutions

VB Engineering has been a simulation partner for many industries over a decade, While simulation is widely used in product design and verification, it is often overlooked in the subsequent stage of the value chain manufacturing. This missed opportunity prevents organizations from studying the behavior of their manufacturing processes and systems before deployment. Given the high costs and capital-intensive nature of commissioning new manufacturing facilities, production lines, and processes, integrating simulation methods into manufacturing can offer significant benefits. VB Engineering helped factories to optimize their processes by identifying bottlenecks, inefficiencies, and areas for improvement. By simulating different scenarios, factories can test and refine their processes to increase productivity, reduce cycle times, and improve overall efficiency.
