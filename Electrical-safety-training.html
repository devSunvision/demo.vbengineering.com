Process We Follow

Electrical safety training and skill up program is conducted by VB to clients with a clear motive of creating the awareness on electrical risk and their handling methods. The only aim of “Safety First” drive this entire course. We conduct electrical safety training both on site and online as per client requirements. The training also conducted with two levels of certification for the trainees. The trainees who participate in the program will be awarded with participation certification. We enable the trainees to be more skilled and qualified and evaluate them at different levels. The trainees will be appearing for different levels of assessment for electrical safety and once they clear it they will be awarded with Bronze, Silver and gold certificate respectively.

Electrical Safety Training Program needs a facility level customization. Our team in well advance will work with your team to prepare a custom electrical safety program. We have prebuild electrical safety curriculum and material which whill start the electrical safety awareness from the basics. The intensified electrical safety training program is not just limited to class lectures and a field inspection along with the site team will be envisaged. The field training will help the trainees to understand the electrical hazards present in the work place and the way of dealing with these electrical risks.

The Electrical Safety Training program was built in different levels starting from the one who needs basic awareness of the various electrical risks in the system to a very advanced level. The Program was build by our professional team who has more than 40 years of experience on the filed. The Safety program also involves interactive animation videos which are self-explanatory and easily understandable for the trainee. The strive for making safety a culture can be built from scratch from our training programs. Our advance Virtual reality training programs on safety will also help the users to interact with the real time scenarios in a virtual world and learn the operation in a safer environment.

Electrical safety Training or NFPA training is Particular for professionals operating in electrical areas or working on electrical equipment. A large number of electrocutions, burns, or electrical shock happens because of electrical safety standards weren’t adhered to.

The governance over electrical safety with in the United States rests with NFPA 70E, Standards for Electrical Safety Requirements for workers in Workplace; U.S. Department of Energy; and also OSHA. In Canada, Workplace electrical safety is mandated by CSA Z462 workplace Safety and administered by provincial ministries of labor.

Arc flash safety training or OSHA Safety training needs to measure established who are worked in hazardous area due to high risk of electric shock or electrocution. In United States all electrical work practices must comply with the National Electrical Safety Code, the National Electrical Code, National fire Protection Association (NFPA), OSHA compliance, and U.S. state-adopted electrical safety requirements.
