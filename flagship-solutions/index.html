Questions we hear

Flagship Solutions · FAQ.

Four short answers before you fill the form.

What is a VB Engineering Flagship Solution?

A Flagship Solution is a productized engineering program bundled with a 5-year retainer. Every Flagship is scoped as a fixed baseline in Year 1 followed by annual refresh cycles in Years 2 to 5, signed by a Chartered Engineer on every deliverable.

How many Flagship Solutions does VB Engineering offer?

Four · VB Arc360 (arc flash compliance), VB SafeWatch (process safety HAZOP + SIL revalidation), VB AssetTwin (LiDAR to digital twin) and VB PowerPulse (10-study PSS + relay setting files + annual re-tune).

What differentiates a Flagship from a standard engineering service?

Flagships are productized (fixed scope, fixed cadence), bundled with a 5-year retainer, backed by software enforcement where relevant, and signed by two Chartered Engineers per deliverable. Standard services are project-scoped and single-signature.

Can a Flagship replace an existing multi-vendor arrangement?

Yes. The most common Flagship engagement replaces a fragmented arrangement of separate consultants with a single VB retainer, one signature, one Business Head owner.
