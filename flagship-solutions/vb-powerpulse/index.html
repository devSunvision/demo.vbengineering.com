The problem

A PSS report on the shelf is a plant waiting to trip.

Stage 01 · MODEL

The model has to match the plant.

Electrical single-line diagrams are walked down and reconciled against the physical switchgear. Load census done from CMMS + walkdown, not just design data. Sources · utility, generators, UPS, batteries · characterised with vendor curves.

The result is an ETAP or DigSILENT model that behaves like the plant · not the plant the design office remembered. Every subsequent study runs on this baseline. Every retainer year starts here.

What ships in this stage
  • Reconciled electrical single-line · issued back to plant
  • Load census · from CMMS + walkdown + measurement
  • Source characterisation · utility + gen + UPS + battery
  • Baseline ETAP / DigSILENT model · version-controlled
  • CEng MIE model acceptance certificate
Stage 02 · STUDY

Ten studies · one integrated report.

The 10 studies · Load Flow, Short Circuit, Protection Coordination, Arc Flash (NFPA 70E + IEEE 1584), Harmonics (IEEE 519), Motor Starting, Transient Stability, Reliability, Insulation Coordination, DC Arc Flash · are run against the same baseline model, in a single cycle, with cases cross-referenced.

Where one study drives another · protection coordination follows short circuit; arc flash follows both · the dependency is documented, not lost between vendors. The report is one signed document, not ten PDFs from three consultants.

What ships in this stage
  • Load flow report · all operating scenarios
  • Short circuit report · IEC 60909 + IEEE 141
  • Protection coordination · time-current curves + margins
  • Arc flash study · NFPA 70E + IEEE 1584 + label register
  • Harmonics + motor starting + stability + reliability + insulation + DC AF
Stage 03 · SET

From report page to relay memory.

Numerical relay settings do not stop at a report table. VB PowerPulse issues the setting file · in the vendor-native format · for every numerical relay in scope. SEL, Siemens, ABB, GE, Vamp, Schneider, Areva · each vendor family, each firmware version, each communication protocol.

The setting files are handed with a commissioning-grade upload checklist and a field verification protocol. The client protection engineer uploads · does not re-key. Every value in the file traces back to a case in the PSS report.

What ships in this stage
  • Relay setting file per relay · vendor-native format
  • Setting summary table · human-readable
  • Upload checklist · one per relay
  • Field verification protocol · primary + secondary injection
  • Setting file register · traceable to PSS case
Stage 04 · VALIDATE

Settings are not right until tested.

Field commissioning support · primary injection where feasible, secondary injection where not. Every setting witnessed. Every discrepancy between file and relay memory logged and resolved. Every test report signed and attached to the PSS case log.

Where a relay does not accept a setting · firmware limit, hardware limit, mis-communicated version · the study is re-run to the operable band. Not the other way around. The plant does not compromise safety to fit an old relay.

What ships in this stage
  • Primary injection test reports · one per relay
  • Secondary injection test reports · where primary not feasible
  • Field commissioning discrepancy log · closed to zero
  • Re-run PSS cases (if any) · CEng MIE signed
  • Client commissioning acceptance certificate
Stage 05 · RETUNE

Steady-state is a discipline, not a state.

Every material change on the electrical system · new feeder, new motor, new renewable tie-in, protection firmware upgrade · triggers a re-run of the affected studies and a re-issue of the affected setting files. The plant electrical engineer does not carry that discipline alone · VB PowerPulse carries it under the retainer.

The 5-year full re-study takes the accumulated MoC deltas and rebases the model, re-runs the 10 studies, re-issues every setting file, and re-signs the report. Year 5 is a signature, not a rebuild · because the annual cadence held.

What ships in this stage
  • Annual re-tune report · scope of changes documented
  • MoC-triggered study re-runs · one per event
  • Setting file re-issue · versioned + audit trail
  • Year 5 full re-study · CEng MIE re-signed

CASE 01 · REFINING

Landmark East Coast refinery · 220 kV to 415 V · 340 relays under VB PowerPulse retainer.

Baseline in Year 1 exposed 42 mis-coordinated pairs across the protection scheme. All 340 numerical relays received new setting files, primary injection tested. Year 3 mid-cycle refresh caught a UPS refresh that would have moved a critical 11 kV feeder outside its coordination margin. Corrected before energisation.

Zero mis-operations across 4 years of retainer
CASE 02 · STEEL

Landmark integrated steel plant · India. Harmonics + motor starting + protection coordination on EAF + LMF + rolling mill.

THD at 132 kV busbar had climbed to 9.8 percent post-EAF expansion · IEEE 519 target was 5 percent. VB PowerPulse ran harmonics + mitigation study, sized C-type filters at three PCCs, and re-ran protection coordination to accommodate filter transients. Post-installation THD landed at 3.6 percent.

IEEE 519 compliance restored · PPA compliance re-secured
CASE 03 · DATA CENTRE

Landmark hyperscale data centre · India. Arc flash + coordination on 33 kV incomer + 415 V rack tier.

Data centre added 40 MW of rack capacity. Arc flash IE at MV switchgear had crept from 12 cal to 22 cal. VB PowerPulse ran arc flash + coordination + protection settings refresh. Recommended relay firmware upgrade + upstream instantaneous element enable. Post-tune IE dropped to 6 cal at MV, 4 cal at 415 V rack tier.

PPE category reduced from 4 to 2 across 78 percent of site
